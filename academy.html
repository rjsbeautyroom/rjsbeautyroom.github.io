    Training FAQ

    Good to know

    Anything else you'd like to ask? Send us a message and we'll get back to you.

    Do I need any experience?

    Not for our Beginner Lash Training. It's designed for complete beginners, and we take you through everything from the theory to working on live models.

    Is it 1:1 or a group?

    Both. Most courses are 1:1, and our Beginner Lash Training is a small group class of just 3, so you still get plenty of attention.

    Will I get a certificate?

    Yes. Our courses are BTA accredited, apart from masterclasses, and the Beginner Lash Training includes a certificate.

    Can I get insured afterwards?

    Yes. The Beginner Lash Training is insurable on completion, so you can start working on clients.

    Is a kit included?

    A full kit is included with the Beginner Lash Training and the classic and volume lash extension courses.

    What support do I get after the course?

    You'll have continued support, digital forever manuals, treatment checklists, client record cards and social media tips to help you get started.

    How do I book?

    Message us on Facebook or email Missrjstutorials@gmail.com and we'll talk you through dates and availability.
