      Ask a question

      How do I book?

      Book online through our booking page, where you can see every treatment and pick a time that suits you. If you have a question first, message us on Facebook.

      Is there a booking fee?

      Yes. A non-refundable booking fee is needed to secure your appointment.

      What if I need to cancel or move my appointment?

      Please give us at least 48 hours' notice. Cancellations within 48 hours are charged at 50% of the treatment price, and missed appointments are charged in full. We know life happens, so just get in touch as early as you can.

      Do I need a patch test?

      For lash extensions, lash lifts, tints and PMU microblading, a patch test may be needed before your first appointment. We'll let you know when you book.

      How should I prepare for lash extensions?

      Come with clean lashes, free of mascara and make-up. Avoid oil-based products around the eyes on the day, and skip caffeine if you find it hard to keep still. You'll lie back with your eyes closed, and most people find it really relaxing.

      How long do lash extensions last, and when do I need an infill?

      Extensions shed naturally with your own lashes. Most clients book an infill every 2 to 3 weeks to keep their set looking full. Following your aftercare helps them last.

      Which lash style should I choose?

      Have a look at our lash style guide. Every set is tailored to your eye shape and the look you want, so if you're not sure, we'll help you decide at your appointment.

      How long does PMU microblading last?

      Approximately 12 to 18 months before a further top-up is needed. Results vary with skin type and lifestyle. Your 8-week top-up is included in the price, and we'll give you full aftercare advice so your brows heal beautifully.

      Can I bring my own tattoo design?

      Of course. Bring your idea, a picture or a reference and we'll create a fine line or minimalist design with you. Tattoo prices start from £30 and depend on the design.

      Where are you based?

      We're in Sheffield. We work by appointment only, and the full address is sent to you when you book.

      Do you run beauty training courses?

      Yes. We offer accredited 1:1 and small group courses in lashes and brows, from beginner lash training to lash lift and brow lamination. See the Training Academy for courses and prices.
